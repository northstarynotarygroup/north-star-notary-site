Our services

We provide in-person mobile notary public services in metro Atlanta, including acknowledgments, oaths and affirmations, jurats, signature witnessing, I-9 employment verification as an authorized representative, witness provision, and apostille preparation and delivery. Every signer must be physically present with the notary. We do not perform remote, video, or online notarizations.

Booking and confirmation

Booking requests are not final until we confirm them. We review each request personally and confirm it in writing. Before we travel to you, we send you your total in writing, including the travel fee and the notarial fee. Please review it. By going ahead with the appointment you accept that total.

Fees and payment

  • Notarial fees. Georgia law sets what a notary may charge for the notarial act itself. We charge $2.00 per notarial act (each signature notarized, oath, or jurat). One notarial act is one signature notarized, not one document or one page. See O.C.G.A. § 45-17-11.
  • Travel and other fees. Georgia law does not set travel charges. Our travel fee and any add-on fees (for example hospital or facility visits, same-day rush, after-hours or holiday appointments, witness provision, apostille runner service, and late arrival) are billed separately from the notarial fee, are listed on our pricing page, and are quoted to you before you book. If anything on our website differs from the written total we send you for your appointment, the written total controls.
  • Deposit. A $20 deposit is charged to your card when you book and applied toward your total. Card payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number.
  • Balance. The remaining balance is due at the appointment and can be paid by cash, Zelle, Cash App, or card.
  • Third-party fees. Government and third-party fees, such as the Georgia Superior Court Clerks' Cooperative Authority apostille filing fee, are separate and are set by those offices.

Cancellations, lateness, and incomplete appointments

  • Your $20 deposit is refunded in full if you cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment. If you cancel with less notice, or do not show up, the deposit is forfeited. The deposit is not a penalty. It covers the time we reserved for you and the cost of holding that slot.
  • If you are more than 15 minutes late, the late fee shown on our pricing page applies.
  • If we arrive and the signing cannot be completed because of an expired or invalid ID, a missing signer, or blank spaces left in the document, the travel fee still applies, because the time was reserved and the visit was made.

What you need to do

  • Every signer must be physically present and show an unexpired government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license, passport, state ID, or military ID. Georgia law requires us to confirm each signer's identity, and we cannot notarize without it.
  • Bring the complete document, unsigned. Please sign in front of us, not beforehand. Fill in every blank except your signature.
  • Bring any witnesses your document requires, or tell us when you book that you would like us to provide one.
  • You are responsible for choosing the right document and for reading it before you sign. You are also responsible for confirming that the person or office receiving it, such as a court, county recorder, lender, employer, or foreign authority, will accept it.
  • For some documents Georgia law requires us to record details in a notary journal, such as your name, address, phone number, and the type of identification you showed. We need that information from you to complete the notarization.

When we may decline

We may decline or stop a notarization if we believe the transaction is illegal, false, or deceptive, if a signer appears to be coerced, or if a signer's demeanor gives us serious doubt that they understand and willingly sign what is in front of them. We also cannot notarize a document in which we are a signer or a party. When we decline for these reasons we are following Georgia law, and this is not a failure on our part to provide the service.

Apostilles

For apostille requests we notarize your document if needed, then deliver it to the Georgia Superior Court Clerks' Cooperative Authority (GSCCCA), which is the office that issues the apostille. We do not issue apostilles and we do not control the GSCCCA's decisions, hours, or processing times. An apostille only works for countries that are part of the Hague Apostille Convention. You are responsible for confirming what the destination country requires. We do not guarantee that a foreign authority will accept your document.
